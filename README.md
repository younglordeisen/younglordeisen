### About

I work self-directed across a wide range of technical disciplines rather than staying in one lane — AI/ML infrastructure and compute architecture, scientific research tooling, fabrication and hardware, professional AV/audio production, and the networking/systems backbone that ties it all together. Most of that work lives on a large multi-campus build I design and manage end-to-end: everything from server racks to studio audio networks to building-level automation.

That range is the point. I'd rather go deep enough in each layer of a stack — network, compute, software, physical build — to actually reason about how they affect each other, instead of only knowing my own layer.
